# Northwind Robotics: Q3 2026 Report

A fictional company report used as the lab's sample knowledge base.

## Financial Summary

Q3 revenue was $4.2M, up 18% from Q2's $3.56M. Gross margin held at 61%.
Operating expenses rose to $3.9M, driven mainly by hiring in engineering.
The company ended the quarter with $11.4M in cash, about 22 months of runway at the current burn.

## Product

The Mk-4 warehouse picker shipped to 9 new customers, bringing the installed base to 47 sites.
Mean time between failures improved from 310 hours to 420 hours after the gripper redesign.
The Mk-5 prototype is in field trials at two sites; general availability is planned for Q2 2027.

## Customers

Net revenue retention was 124%. Two customers churned, both citing integration effort with legacy
warehouse management systems. The largest customer, Hadley Logistics, expanded from 6 to 14 units.

## Team

Headcount grew from 58 to 71. Engineering is now 39 people. The VP of Sales role has been open
since July. Voluntary attrition for the trailing twelve months was 7%.

## Risks

- Supplier concentration: one vendor provides all servo motors for the Mk-4.
- Integration effort is the leading cause of churn and lengthens sales cycles.
- The open VP of Sales role may slow the Q4 pipeline.

## Outlook

Q4 revenue guidance is $4.6M to $4.9M. The company plans to qualify a second servo supplier
by the end of Q1 2027 and to release a no-code integration toolkit in Q1 2027.
