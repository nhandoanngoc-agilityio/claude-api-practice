# Northwind Robotics: Engineer Onboarding Guide

## First Week

Day one is laptop setup and a safety briefing for the test floor. Every engineer pairs with a
buddy for the first two weeks. Ship one small change to production by the end of week one.

## Test Floor Safety

Robots on the floor run in supervised mode only. Wear the issued badge so the safety scanner
can track you. Never disable an e-stop, even for a demo.

## Releases

Firmware releases ship every second Tuesday. A release needs two approvals and a passing
hardware-in-the-loop run. Hotfixes need one approval and a written rollback plan.

## Who To Ask

- Firmware questions: the #firmware channel.
- Floor access and safety: the facilities lead.
- Payroll and benefits: the people team.
